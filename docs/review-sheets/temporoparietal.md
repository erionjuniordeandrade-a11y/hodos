### Debrief 1: Use a pathway hypothesis, not a tract diagnosis

**Claim:** Saur and colleagues combined task fMRI and tractography to investigate repetition and comprehension routes. Their task-based dorsal/ventral account offers a framework for discussion. It does not turn this fictional task profile into proof of an isolated fascicle lesion.

**Claim:** Work on temporal-resection cohorts combined tractography with visual-field outcomes and described variation in Meyer’s loop.
