# What the anatomy image can establish

Lesson id: `evidence-classes` · Minutes: 8 · Review status: draft

## Opening question

A highlighted parcel, a nearby tract and weakness appear in one report. What does each actually establish?

## Three takeaways

1. Identify whether the object is a surface landmark, parcel label or reconstruction. _(step 0: A landmark and a parcel answer different questions)_
2. Attach a functional statement to its measurement and method. _(step 2: Put the task beside a functional claim)_
3. Keep atlas anatomy, reconstructed course and examination findings distinguishable. _(step 3: Construct a statement with the correct scope)_

## Steps

### Step 0: A landmark and a parcel answer different questions

**Claim:** A sulcus describes surface shape. A parcel divides cortex according to an atlas method. The two can be anatomically related without sharing the same boundary.

**Sources:**
- **M1** — Glasser et al., 2016 · multi-modal parcellation of human cerebral cortex (PMID 27437579) — atlas
- **R1** — Frigeri et al., 2015 · microsurgical anatomy of the central lobe (PMID 25555079) — experimental_anatomy

### Step 1: A plausible course is an anatomical hypothesis

**Claim:** The CST sample provides a three-dimensional trajectory to inspect. Its appearance can support a comparison with expected anatomy, but it does not verify every connection.

**Sources:**
