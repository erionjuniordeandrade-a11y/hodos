# Salience network · insula, cingulate & switching

Lesson id: `salience-network` · Minutes: 18 · Review status: draft

## Opening question

After a frontoinsular procedure, redirecting behaviour toward relevant events is difficult. What must a useful explanation include?

## Three takeaways

1. Locate frontoinsular and dorsal cingulate reference territories. _(step 0: Anchor the discussion in anterior insula and cingulate cortex)_
2. Keep salience, ventral attention and cingulo-opercular definitions separate. _(step 2: Separate three overlapping network vocabularies)_
3. Describe the behaviour and task before advancing a switching or lesion-network hypothesis. _(step 5: Present a behavioural change before naming its network)_

## Steps

### Step 0: Anchor the discussion in anterior insula and cingulate cortex

**Claim:** Seeley’s salience-network account linked frontoinsular and dorsal anterior cingulate regions with broader limbic/subcortical relationships. Begin with the buried lateral and exposed medial cortical anatomy.

### Step 2: Separate three overlapping network vocabularies

**Claim:** Salience, ventral attention and cingulo-opercular control are related descriptions with different defining studies. Treating the names as exact synonyms hides differences in cortical coverage, tasks and analysis scale.
