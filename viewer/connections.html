      Reading the map

      Each link is a sentence from the author’s white matter teaching notes, extracted into subject, relation and object, and checked to be quoted verbatim. The map shows what the notes state. It is not a tractography result, a measured connectivity, or a list of every connection a structure has.

      Teaching case vignettes from the same notes are not shown. Educational draft awaiting independent clinical review; not for patient-specific planning. For reference anatomy in three dimensions, open the atlas.
