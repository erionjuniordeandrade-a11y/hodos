Intraoperative testing guide · Educational draft

How to test it. Monitoring vs mapping.

Mapping asks whether one specific site is essential for an active task right now. Monitoring asks whether a pathway is still conducting or systemic physiology is stable. Preoperative fMRI, DTI, and nTMS are planning priors, not intraoperative proof.

Monitoring vs mapping

Do not confuse asleep surgery with unmapped surgery, and never treat a monitoring signal as a focal map or vice versa.

Task timing: 4-second DES vs continuous monitoring

The DuLIP battery separates timed <=4 s stimulation trials with carrier phrases from unpaced resection monitoring. Tasks whose normative baseline delays exceed 4 s belong in continuous monitoring, not pass/fail DES.
