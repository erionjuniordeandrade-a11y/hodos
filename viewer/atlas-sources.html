This is group reference anatomy. The surfaces and pathways come from different atlas constructions using MNI coordinate conventions. Their juxtaposition is illustrative: no quantitative cross-atlas distance, endpoint connectivity or patient registration is claimed. Lesson text is an educational draft awaiting anatomical review. Research only · not navigation: nothing on these pages is registered to a patient or fit for surgical planning.

Cortical surface and parcels

HCP S1200 group-average midthickness surface, fs_LR 32k, MSMAll; 32,492 vertices per hemisphere. HCP-MMP1 labels use matching compressed vertex order and separate left/right tables. Clicking reports the nearest vertex of a surface triangle, without interpolating categorical labels.

Van Essen et al., 2013 · Glasser et al., 2016 · HCP Open Access Data Use Terms

Resting-state networks

Yeo-7 and Yeo-17 intrinsic functional connectivity networks (Yeo et al., 2011), estimated by clustering resting-state fMRI from 1,000 healthy adults and carried here as per-vertex labels on the same fs_LR 32k surface (resampled by the DiedrichsenLab fs_LR_32 project). A network is a population cluster of correlated signal at rest: it is not a task map, not a lesion-deficit map, and it says nothing about any individual patient. "Salience" in this viewer is the Yeo-7 ventral attention network, which finer parcellations split into salience and ventral-attention components. Only the 7-network set is exposed in the interface; the 17-network labels ship for later use. The network reported for a click is the label at that vertex, never an inference for the whole parcel.

Yeo et al., 2011 · PMID 21653723 · fs_LR 32k label files · FreeSurfer atlas license

Sampled reference pathways

HCP1065 population-average tractography, Yeh 2022. The reference preparation samples at most 220 streamlines per bundle and resamples each to 28 points. These are display paths, not axons or patient reconstructions. No tract length, conduction velocity or latency is inferred from their animation. Paired traces are a direction-neutral teaching effect.

Yeh, 2022 · Original atlas and terms · CC BY-SA 4.0. The redistributed tract binary and metadata retain this license.

Deep and inferior context

Melbourne Subcortex scale 1 supplies gross reference structures. Surfaces were extracted and smoothed by the reference pipeline; anterior/posterior thalamic regions are merged. No precise relay nucleus is identified. The inferior context mesh is a remainder of the MNI152NLin2009cAsym brain mask after cortical exclusion, not a named cerebellar or brainstem segmentation.

Tian et al., 2020 · Melbourne atlas license · Fonov et al., 2011 · MNI permission notice

Dissection reference photographs

Five photographs from Dr. Erion de Andrade’s supplied glioma-connectome lecture accompany selected Compare steps. They show left lateral dissections with anterior at image left. Original highlights and watermarks are preserved. The specimen photographs and the group atlas have independent geometry, viewpoints and colour conventions. The photographs do not supply patient registration, functional measurements, surgical margins or missing 3D layers.
