Fourteen lessons. Orient, compare, explain.

Each lesson runs on the live atlas. You locate the structures, put the neighbours in the same view, and answer a resident-level question before the explanation and its sources are revealed. Lessons run 8 to 22 minutes, depending on how many relationships each one covers.

Hodos is the Greek word for path, the root of hodotopy: function lives in the pathways as much as in the cortex.
